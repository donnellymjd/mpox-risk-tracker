Method

This unofficial tracker fetches public NYC Health mpox case data, smooths daily-equivalent cases with a 14-day Gaussian window, and converts the day-to-day change in smoothed log cases into a normalized spread indicator. Weekly case counts are distributed across their reporting week before smoothing.

It is intended for situational awareness, not clinical or individual medical guidance. Follow NYC Health guidance for prevention, testing, vaccination, and treatment decisions.
