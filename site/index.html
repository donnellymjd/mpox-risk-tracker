Method

This unofficial tracker fetches public NYC Health mpox case data. Its weekly growth trend fits eight consecutive seven-day case totals on a log(1 + cases) scale. The chart updates the fit daily where cases are reported daily and weekly where only weekly totals exist. Weekly totals are distributed across reporting days only for the daily-equivalent and cumulative case views, not for the current growth trend.

It is intended for situational awareness, not clinical or individual medical guidance. Follow NYC Health guidance for prevention, testing, vaccination, and treatment decisions.
